03 / Validation Suite

Statistical Robustness Checks

Every RDD estimate is tested against all four checks before it reaches the dashboard. This separates a defensible causal claim from a toy model.

Bandwidth Sensitivity
✅ Robust (15–30%)
Effect does not flip sign or lose significance across 4 bandwidths. Re-run at 10%, 15%, 20%, 25% — same conclusion.
Placebo / Falsification
✅ No effect at fake cutoffs
Identical RDD at −10%, −5%, +5% cutoff produces near-zero, insignificant "effects" — the −19% result isn't an artifact.
McCrary Density Test
✅ No manipulation detected
No discontinuity in the density of the running variable at cutoff — districts aren't sorting around the classification threshold.
Covariate Balance
✅ Pre-treatment balance
Observable pre-treatment characteristics (prior prices, market count) don't jump at the cutoff — valid identification.
